MUET Quick Intro
  • Malaysian University English Test by MPM.
  • 4 components: Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing.
›

Used for university entry and academic English readiness. Result valid for 5 years. Sessions: 1, 2, 3 and MUET on Demand (MoD).

Why Take MUET?
  • Required for Malaysian higher education admission.
  • Proves English proficiency for study and career.
›

Many universities require Band 3 or 4 for degree entry. A higher band strengthens scholarship and postgraduate applications.

Important MUET Facts
  • 360 marks total · 90 per component.
  • Band 1–5+ · Result valid 5 years.
