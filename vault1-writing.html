Situation:
You received an email from your friend, Aina, who wants to organise a study programme for MUET candidates during the semester break.
  • Suggest one suitable activity.
  • Explain why the activity would help MUET candidates.
  • Suggest a suitable place for the programme.
  • Tell Aina whether you can help organise it.
Need Task 1 language support?

Open: “Thank you for your email…”
Suggest: “I think we should…”
Explain: “This would be useful because…”
Close: “I hope these suggestions help. Best wishes,…”
