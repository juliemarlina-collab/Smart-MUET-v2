01 · Background & journey

Why it was created

MUET preparation can feel fragmented: learners need to understand the test, find useful strategies, practise each component and interpret their results. Smart MUET Guide brings those steps into one guided journey for independent practice and review.

How the project has developed

  1. Teaching and component practiceLearning activities for the four MUET skills informed the guided approach.
  2. Smart MUET Guide V2 · 2026The web app brings guidance, two Practice Vaults, Mock Practice 1 and progress tracking into a connected flow.
  3. Current phase · user testingLearners and educators are invited to try the app and point out what should be improved before wider use.

02 · Functions & features

01

Understand the exam

Start with MUET information and component guides for Speaking, Reading, Listening and Writing.

02

Learn strategies

Move from explanations to targeted activities with a clear next step for each skill.

03

Practise in stages

Vault 1 offers guided practice; Vault 2 supports more independent practice.

04

Try a mock

Mock Practice 1 brings all four components together in an exam-style stage.

05

Review progress

See completed component scores, a stage total and a practice band in the progress view.

06

Reflect and improve

Practice feedback and Smart Coach prompts support reflection in Speaking and Writing.

03 · User testing information

Help us make the experience better.

This online testing phase examines usability, clarity of instructions, suitability of content and the learning experience across devices. It is an opportunity to find issues and improve the app.

01

Open the app

Create a profile if you are a new learner, or continue with your existing profile.

02

Explore and practise

Read the guides and try the Vault and Mock components relevant to you.

03

Check the experience

Notice unclear questions, audio or display issues, accessibility barriers and misleading feedback.

04

Share feedback

Complete the student or educator questionnaire and name the page or component in your suggestions.

For learners

Tell us what helped you learn, where you felt confused and whether the instructions and feedback were clear.

For lecturers & teachers

Review content suitability, scaffolding, scoring explanations, accessibility and classroom readiness. You can review without submitting student answers.

Scores and automated Speaking or Writing feedback are practice estimates, not official MUET results. Questionnaire responses are collected separately from practice attempts.
