One memorable technique. Four simple moves.

S — STUDY: Study the question first so you know exactly what you are looking for.
1Study the question before searching the text.
2Expect paraphrases rather than exact wording.
3Choose answers supported by evidence, not assumptions.

Mini Reading Situation

Question: Why did the programme expand? Text: “Demand increased sharply, prompting organisers to add two extra sessions.”
