Words made simple.

Short explanations for the terms you will meet during practice.

MUET

Malaysian University English Test. It assesses Listening, Speaking, Reading and Writing.

MPM

Majlis Peperiksaan Malaysia (Malaysian Examinations Council), the body responsible for official MUET information.

Paper code, such as 800/1

An exam document or paper identifier. Read the nearby title to see which component it refers to; it is not your mark.

CEFR

Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. It describes language ability in broad levels, such as B1 and B2.

Band estimate

A practice indication based on your combined score out of 360. It is not an official MUET band.

Component score

Your practice score for one skill, shown out of 90. Your overall practice score combines four components out of 360.

Task Fulfilment

How well your response answers all parts of the question and develops relevant ideas.

Coherence and Organisation

How clearly ideas are arranged and connected so a reader or listener can follow them.

Register

The level of formality you use. A reply to a friend sounds different from a formal report.

PREP

Point → Reason → Example → Point. A simple way to develop a Speaking presentation idea.

PEEL

Point → Explain → Example → Link. A planning pattern for an essay body paragraph.

SCAN and CLUE

Reading and Listening reminders in the Guide. Use their full steps there to find evidence before choosing an answer.

Vault 1 / Vault 2

Guided practice comes first in Vault 1. Vault 2 lets you practise more independently.

Mock test

A practice test that helps you experience exam-style timing and questions. Its result is still a practice estimate.

Database receipt

Confirmation that an attempt reached the pilot database. A result saved on your device can appear before this confirmation.
