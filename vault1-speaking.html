Tips & Tricks
PREP strategy before you practise
▼
PPoint
RReason
EExample
PPoint
again
P — Point: State your stand clearly in sentence 1
Start with a complete opinion. The examiner decides your band within the first sentence.
Use: “I believe…” · “In my opinion…” · “I strongly feel that…”
R — Reason: Explain why — one strong reason only
One strong reason scores more than three weak ones.
Use: “This is because…” · “The reason is…” · “Due to the fact that…”
E — Example: Elaborate with a Malaysian example
Local examples score better. Use PTPTN, campus life, EPF, Malaysian companies.
Use: “For example…” · “Take Malaysia as an example…”
P — Point Again: Conclude confidently
Restate your stand. This signals the examiner you have finished your response cleanly.
Use: “Therefore, I strongly believe that…” · “In conclusion…”

👥 TASK B — GROUP DISCUSSION TIPS

🎯Initiate: Be the first to speak. “Shall we begin by looking at the first option?”
🗣Prompt: Ask others. “What do you think, [name]?” · “[Name], do you agree?”
⚡Turn-take: “Building on what [name] said…” · “I would like to add…”
⚖️Negotiate: “That is a valid point, however…” · “I see it differently because…”
✅Conclude: Help the group decide. “So as a group, we have decided that the best option is…”

Quick Reminders

🎯One strong reason beats three weak ones
🇲🇾Use Malaysian examples: PTPTN, campus life, EPF, cost of living in KL
🚫Replace fillers (erm, like, you know) with a 1-second pause
👤Task B: refer to others by name at least twice

Sample topic: Salary as reason for choosing a job

P — Point (Expressing opinion)I believe salary is the most important reason when choosing a job because it directly determines financial security.
R — Reason (Giving reasons / Justifying)A good salary allows a graduate to repay education loans, cover living expenses and begin saving — all at once.
E — Example (Elaborating)Many Malaysian graduates with PTPTN loans must prioritise salary because repayment starts six months after graduation. A salary below RM2,000 in KL makes this extremely difficult.
P — Point Again (Concluding / Summarising)Therefore, salary is the most critical factor as it forms the foundation of financial independence.
📝 EXAMINER NOTE

Direct answer · one clear reason · specific local example · strong closing. About 90 seconds. No fillers. This response demonstrates: expressing opinions, giving reasons, elaborating and concluding — all 4 key language functions.

Phrase Bank
Ready-made phrases for Task A & Task B
▼
🗣 TASK A — EXPRESSING YOUR POINT (P)
“I believe that…” · “In my opinion…” · “I strongly feel that…”
“From my perspective…” · “I would argue that…”
💡 GIVING YOUR REASON (R)
“This is because…” · “The reason is…” · “Due to the fact that…”
“This can be attributed to…” · “One major reason is…”
🏭 ELABORATING WITH AN EXAMPLE (E)
“For example…” · “For instance…” · “A clear example of this is…”
“Take Malaysia as an example…” · “To illustrate this point…”
✅ CONCLUDING (P again)
“Therefore, I strongly believe that…” · “In conclusion…”
“To summarise my point…” · “This is why I feel that…”
👥 TASK B — INTERACTION PHRASES
Initiate: “Shall we start by looking at…?”
Prompt: “What do you think, [name]?” · “[Name], do you agree?”
Turn-take: “Building on what [name] said…” · “I would like to add…”
Negotiate: “That is a valid point, however…”
Conclude: “So as a group, we have decided…”
❌ AVOID THESE FILLERS
erm… · like… · you know… · so yeah… · actually actually…
Replace with a 1-second pause. A pause sounds confident. Fillers lose marks.
