MUET 800/2 · SPEAKING

PREP your way to a stronger response.

See it. Tap it. Learn it. Use PREP to organise ideas quickly before you move into speaking practice.

Tap PREP to learn the structure

One memorable technique. Four simple moves.

POINT: State your main idea clearly and directly.

Individual Presentation

Organise one clear idea and develop it logically.

1Read the task carefully.
2Plan keywords, not a script.
3Use PREP to develop your idea.

Group Discussion

Use shorter PREP turns and interact naturally with others.

1Listen before responding.
2Build on or challenge ideas politely.
3Help the group reach a conclusion.

Example Topic

Universities should encourage students to join volunteer activities.

Tap a PREP letter to reveal that part of the answer.
Opinion: In my opinion… / I believe that…
Reason: This is because… / One important reason is…
Example: For example… / For instance…
Interact: Building on that point… / I see your point, however…
